# Music Royalty NFTs - Revenue sharing for music creators

## Project Description
This project is a decentralized platform enabling music creators to mint NFTs that represent their music rights. It facilitates transparent and automated revenue sharing between the original creators and NFT holders. Revenue from music streams, sales, or licenses can be deposited into the contract and distributed fairly among stakeholders based on predefined royalty percentages and shares.

## Project Vision
Our vision is to empower music creators by leveraging blockchain technology to automate royalty payments and revenue sharing. This transparent, trustless system guarantees artists receive fair compensation while allowing fans and collaborators to participate in the revenue ecosystem through NFTs.

## Key Features
- **NFT Minting:** Creators can mint NFTs representing ownership or rights to music.
- **Revenue Sharing:** Automatically splits revenue between creators and NFT holders based on agreed shares.
- **Secure Withdrawals:** Both creators and holders can safely withdraw their earned revenue.
- **Transparent Accounting:** All transactions and revenue splits are recorded on the blockchain for full transparency.

## Future Scope
- Integrate with ERC721/ERC1155 NFT standards for enhanced interoperability.
- Add metadata and decentralized storage (e.g., IPFS) for music files and related content.
- Support multiple creators per NFT for collaborative projects.
- Automate revenue streams by integrating with streaming platforms.
- Introduce on-chain governance for community-driven decisions on revenue splits.
- Implement secondary sale royalties to benefit creators on resale of NFTs.
